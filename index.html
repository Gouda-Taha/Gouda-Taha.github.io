A Little About Me

I am an applied linguist with more than four years of research on generative AI in language education, human–AI interaction, and second language writing. My work combines classroom-based mixed-methods research, discourse analysis, qualitative coding, and Python-based text analysis.

My Research Agenda

My research examines how students and teachers use generative AI in writing and language learning, and how feedback from AI is processed, evaluated, and taken up.

Drawing on applied and computational linguistics, my work focuses on three interconnected strands:

  • AI-mediated feedback pedagogy: designing classroom practices and feedback-processing training that help students revise more effectively with generative AI.
  • Human–AI interaction in writing: understanding how students collaboratively evaluate, negotiate, and incorporate ChatGPT-generated feedback, and how teachers integrate it into instruction.
  • Methods for analyzing interaction data: developing qualitative coding schemes and using discourse analysis and Python-based text analysis to study peer interaction, feedback uptake, and revision.

This work has led to a co-authored book with Cambridge University Press and several peer-reviewed articles, and I have presented at regional, national, and international conferences.

Projects

My recent projects include:

  • Dissertation project: a classroom-based study of how first-year writing students collaboratively process ChatGPT-generated feedback. It combines recorded group discussions, original and revised drafts, AI feedback, written reflections, interviews, and two-rater writing assessments.
  • GenAI in language teaching: collaborative research with colleagues in the Applied Linguistics Lab on teachers’ and graduate students’ use of ChatGPT in instruction.
  • Undergraduate research mentorship: guiding first-year writing students through discourse-community research and public presentation at the university-wide Learning Showcase.

Teaching

At East Texas A&M University I teach First-Year Writing (ENG 100, 1301, 1302) and Language Acquisition and Processing (ENG 462). Earlier, I taught linguistics and English for specific purposes at Badr University in Cairo and English courses at the American University in Cairo.
